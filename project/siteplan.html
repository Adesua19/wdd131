Website Planning Document

CareConnect Nigeria

A Guide to Telemedicine & Digital Health

1. Site Name

Site Name: CareConnect Nigeria

Reason: The name reflects a clear, approachable hub connecting everyday users in Nigeria with reliable information on virtual consultations, online pharmacies, and diagnostic labs.

Optional Domain: careconnectng.org

2. Site Purpose

This site gives visitors a straightforward overview of how telemedicine works in Nigeria, why it helps save time and travel stress, and practical tips for safe virtual consultations. It will also feature a filterable Telehealth Directory with a localStorage-powered "My Care List," a "Suggest & Ask" contact form, and an unstyled references page linked in the footer.

3. Scenarios

  • How does a virtual consultation work in Nigeria, and how can I prepare before my appointment?
  • How can I filter telehealth platforms, online pharmacies, or lab services by care type and save favorites to "My Care List"?
  • How can I suggest a trusted digital health service or ask a question about getting started?

4. Color Schema

These colors are defined below and applied directly across this planning document:

#0F6E6E

Primary Teal — Header, footer, main headings, and accent borders

#1E293B

Deep Slate — Primary body text and high-contrast labels

#F4F8F8

Soft Mist — Page background and wireframe containers

#FFFFFF

Crisp White — Content cards and text on dark backgrounds

5. Typography

  • Plus Jakarta Sans: Used for headings (h1, h2, h3) to create clear structure and visual hierarchy.
  • Inter: Used for body paragraphs, lists, and labels for clean readability across mobile and desktop viewports.
